# 0070. No touch and hold for pointing at the app

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-15
- Decided by: the owner

## Context

Touch and hold collides with scrolling and with what the iPhone does on a long touch.

## Options weighed

Not recorded.

## Decision

Pointing mode on the phone, right click on a computer.

## Consequences

See [0074](0074-no-two-finger-tap.md).
