## Decision

Handled remarks are collapsed as a group behind one "handled" item, not one by one. A remark's actions sit at its top right: one action as a button, two or more under a ⋮ menu, Handle included. The form sends with Ctrl+Enter, and closing it with text in it asks to cancel, close (keeping the text) or close and reset.
