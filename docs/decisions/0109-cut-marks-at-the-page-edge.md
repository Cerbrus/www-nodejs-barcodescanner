# 0109. The sheet of recipe codes has cut marks at the page edge, no lines between the codes

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-23
- Decided by: the owner
- Supersedes: [0103](0103-recipe-codes-printed-on-a4.md)

## Context

The first printed sheet had thin dashed lines between the cells to cut along ([0103](0103-recipe-codes-printed-on-a4.md)). A cut along such a line leaves ink on the edge of the cut-out code.

## Options weighed

- Dashed lines between the cells.
- Marks at the edge of the page only.

## Decision

The sheet stays as in 0103: A4 portrait, three columns by four rows, each cell with the code 50 mm wide and the recipe's name centred below it on at most two lines, black on white, more than twelve recipes on a next page, the same sheet for one recipe.

Where to cut is shown by short marks at the edge of the page, one at each end of every cut: a mark above and below each column boundary and left and right of each row boundary, 3.5 mm long and 1 mm clear of the cells. Nothing is printed between the cells.

The page margin for printing is 5 mm, so the marks fall inside what a printer reaches; the cells keep 10 mm from the paper's edge.

In the home-screen app on an iPhone, which cannot print, the same sheet is drawn as one picture per page and handed to the share sheet.

## Consequences

A cut-out code carries no ink of a line. The cuts run the whole width or height of the page, from mark to mark.
